What you can work on

  • Speed and startsExplosive starts from a stance and short acceleration sprints.
  • AgilityCone patterns, cuts and backpedal-to-break drills.
  • Position skillsRoute running, footwork for linemen, or tackling form.
  • ConditioningRepeated short sprints with game-like rest.

Yes. Type flag football as the sport and drills skip contact work.
