Questions

Can I train basketball without a hoop?

Yes. Say there is no hoop when you set where you are training, and the drills switch to ball handling, footwork, defense and conditioning.

How long should a basketball practice be?

Anything from 20 to 90 minutes works. CogniCoachAI sizes each drill so the session fits the time you pick.

Is it good for youth players?

Yes. The level follows the athlete's age by default and drills get easier or harder from their feedback.
