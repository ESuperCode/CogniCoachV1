A sample 45-minute session

  1. 8 min
    Warm-upLight jog with dynamic stretches, then touches on the ball in a small area.
  2. 10 min
    Ball masterySole rolls, inside-outside touches and cone dribbling at rising speed.
  3. 10 min
    PassingOne- and two-touch passes against a wall or partner, both feet.
  4. 10 min
    FinishingDribble at a cone, cut and shoot into the corners.

    Cool-downEasy jog and stretches for hamstrings, hips and calves.

Questions

Can I do soccer training alone?

Yes. Tell it you are training solo and the drills use a ball, a wall and a few cones.

Does it work for goalkeepers?

Type goalkeeping as what you want to work on and the drills focus on handling, footwork and diving.

How often should I train?

Most players do well with three to four sessions a week. Your dashboard tracks streaks and weekly minutes.
