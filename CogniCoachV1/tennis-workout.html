What you can work on

  • FootworkSplit steps, recovery steps and side-to-side movement.
  • GroundstrokesShadow swings and rally patterns for forehand and backhand.
  • Serve and powerRotation, leg drive and shoulder work for a stronger serve.
  • RecoveryMobility and cool-downs that keep shoulders and hips healthy.

A sample 45-minute session

An example of what CogniCoachAI builds. Yours is written for your level, space and time.

  1. 8 min
    Warm-upJog, carioca and arm circles, then mini-tennis or shadow swings.
  2. 10 min
    FootworkSplit step to first step, side shuffles and recovery to the middle.
  3. 12 min
    GroundstrokesCrosscourt patterns, then down-the-line changes of direction.
  4. 8 min
    ServeToss consistency, then serves to targets in each box.
  5. 7 min
    Cool-downEasy walk, shoulder and hip mobility.

Questions

Can I train tennis without a court?

Yes. Off court the drills switch to footwork, shadow swings and strength for rotation and legs.

Will it help my serve?

Type serve as your focus and the session centres on toss, rotation and shoulder strength.

Is it suitable for juniors?

Yes. The level follows age by default and you can set it yourself.
