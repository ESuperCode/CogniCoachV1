Questions

Is tackle training safe?

Drills build technique step by step and start without contact. Always train contact with a coach.

Can I train rugby alone?

Yes. Solo sessions use handling, speed and conditioning drills.

Does it work for sevens?

Type rugby sevens as the sport for more speed and repeated sprint work.
