In a single 6-lap swim sequence, perform 15m bursts with alternating emphasis: first 15m focuses on a powerful catch and early vertical forearm, the next 15m emphasizes controlled breathing and torso rotation, and finishes with a maximal 15m push-off from the wall using a strong kick and streamlined glide. Between bursts, recover with a light easy-swim to reset breath. This drill builds sprint energy, torso stability, and rotational timing for freestyle sprinting.
Start at the baseline in a defensive stance. Slide laterally to the lane line, then sprint forward to the free throw line and close out with high hands. Backpedal to the baseline and repeat to the other side. Finish each rep with a jump shot from the elbow. This improves lateral quickness, closeout technique and conditioning.
Set up 5 cones in a zig-zag pattern 3 yards apart. Dribble the ball through the cones using the inside and outside of your right foot, keep the ball close, then accelerate past the last cone and strike a shot on goal with your laces. Jog back to the start. This drill develops close control and finishing under fatigue.
From the baseline, split step as the feeder drops the ball, move to your right and hit a topspin forehand crosscourt, recover to the center with shuffle steps, then move left for a two-handed backhand down the line. Repeat for 8 balls. Builds footwork, recovery and shot consistency.
Get in a three-point stance. On the snap, explode out for 5 yards, plant your outside foot and cut 90 degrees to the right, then turn your head and catch a pass with both hands above your eyes. Tuck the ball and sprint upfield. This helps receivers sharpen route breaks.
Partner tosses the ball high. Take a three-step approach left-right-left, swing both arms back, jump off both feet and spike the ball with a high elbow and wrist snap, then land softly on both feet. Recover to the net and block with both hands above the net. This trains approach timing and explosiveness.
In an athletic infield ready position, field a ground ball with your glove out front, funnel it to your chest, crow hop and make a strong overhand throw to first base. Reset and repeat 10 times. Develops clean fielding mechanics.
Perform 30 seconds of high knees, then drop into 10 push-ups, stand up and do 10 bodyweight squats, finishing with 5 burpees. Rest 30 seconds and repeat for 3 rounds. This conditioning circuit builds full body endurance.
